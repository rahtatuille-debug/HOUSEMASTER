# Breach notification templates (draft)

*Three notices for a personal data breach. Timings from the brief and a
practitioner summary [VERIFY all]: the controller (the school) notifies
the ODPC within 72 hours of becoming aware of a breach, giving reasons if
later; the processor (HouseMaster) notifies the controller without delay,
and where practicable within 48 hours (the DPA draft commits HouseMaster to
24 hours); individuals are told where the risk to them is high. How to
handle the incident itself: docs/RUNBOOK.md.*

*Fill in what you know; say "not yet known" rather than guessing, and send
an update when you learn more. Never put the affected people's personal
data in the notice to the ODPC beyond what it asks for.*

---

## 1. HouseMaster to the school (processor to controller)

**To:** [head and data protection officer of the school], by email and phone
**Subject:** Personal data breach affecting [School name]'s data in HouseMaster ([date])

Dear [name],

We are writing to tell you about a personal data breach affecting your
school's information in HouseMaster, as our data processing agreement
requires. You are the controller; the decision on notifying the Office of
the Data Protection Commissioner and the people affected is yours, and we
will give you everything you need for it.

1. **What happened:** [plain description].
2. **When:** it started [date/time, or "not yet known"]; we found out on
   [date/time]; it was contained on [date/time, or "not yet"].
3. **How we found out:** [monitoring alert, report from a user...].
4. **Whose data, and how many people:** [pupils / parents / staff; number,
   or best estimate].
5. **What data:** [categories, for example names and class lists; grades;
   health notes...].
6. **Likely consequences for the people affected:** [our assessment].
7. **What we have done:** [containment and recovery steps].
8. **What we recommend you do:** [for example tell parents; reset
   passwords].
9. **Your contact at HouseMaster:** [name, phone, email]. We will update you
   by [time].

[Name], [role], [HouseMaster legal entity]

---

## 2. The school to the ODPC

*Submit through the ODPC's breach notification channel [VERIFY: portal or
email, and any official form, which takes precedence over this template].
The information below follows the items the brief lists the ODPC as
asking for [VERIFY].*

**Controller:** [School legal name], [address], ODPC registration no. [..]
**Contact:** [name, role, phone, email]
**Processor involved:** [HouseMaster legal entity], [contact]

1. **How and when the breach was discovered:** [..]
2. **Chronology:** [date/time the breach began, was discovered, was
   contained, this notification]. If more than 72 hours after becoming
   aware, **reasons for the delay:** [..]
3. **How it occurred:** [cause, as far as known]
4. **Number of data subjects affected:** [number or estimate] ([pupils /
   parents / staff])
5. **Classes of personal data:** [..] (state whether any is sensitive, for
   example health notes)
6. **Potential harm to data subjects:** [..]
7. **Measures taken or proposed** to address the breach and reduce its
   effects: [..]
8. **What affected individuals can do to protect themselves:** [..]
9. **Whether individuals have been, or will be, notified,** and how: [..]

---

## 3. The school to the people affected

*Plain language. For a pupil, write to the parent or guardian.*

**Subject:** Important: your information [or: your child's information] at [School name]

Dear [name],

We're writing to let you know that some of [your / your child's]
information held in our school system was [accessed by someone who should
not have seen it / lost / changed] on [date].

**What happened:** [one or two sentences].

**Which information:** [for example your child's name, class and grades].
[Not affected: for example passwords, health notes.]

**What this could mean for you:** [honest, specific].

**What we have done:** [..]

**What you can do:** [for example be wary of messages claiming to be from
the school asking for information or payment; change your HouseMaster
password (Forgot password on the sign-in page)].

**Questions:** [school contact, phone, email]. You can also contact the
Office of the Data Protection Commissioner [contact, VERIFY].

We are sorry this happened.

[Name], [role], [School name]
