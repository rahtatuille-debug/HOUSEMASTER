# Registering with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (draft guide)

*A step-by-step for the owner. The ODPC's registration regulations,
guidance note and form could not be opened while this was written, so every
requirement below is from the brief and marked [VERIFY]. Check the ODPC's
current registration page and form before starting; they may have
changed.*

## 1. Decide the capacity

- The brief says registration has been mandatory since **14 July 2022**
  [VERIFY], and that the small-entity exemption (turnover under KES 5
  million **and** fewer than 10 employees) does **not** apply to processing
  for listed purposes, which include **operating an educational
  institution** [VERIFY].
- It isn't spelled out whether a vendor serving schools is covered by that
  purpose [VERIFY]. **Safe reading:** register.
- **As a processor**: for the schools' data (pupils, parents, staff).
- **As a controller as well**: for HouseMaster's own-purpose data (school
  administrators' accounts, billing, website enquiries, error telemetry).
- Question 1 in [QUESTIONS_FOR_COUNSEL.md](QUESTIONS_FOR_COUNSEL.md).

## 2. Decide who registers

The legal entity that signs the schools' agreements (question 9): sole
proprietor or company. Have its registration documents ready.

## 3. Prepare

What the application is likely to ask for [VERIFY against the current
form (the brief calls it DPR 1)]:

| Item | Where to get it |
|---|---|
| Name, registration number, address, contacts of the entity | Company or business registration |
| Name and contacts of the person responsible for data protection (DPO, if appointed) | Your choice |
| The purposes of processing | DPA Annex A "Nature and purposes"; the HouseMaster privacy notice |
| The categories of data subjects and of personal data | DPA Annex A |
| Whether sensitive data is processed | Probably yes: pupils' health notes, possibly family relationships [VERIFY: question 4] |
| Recipients, and transfers outside Kenya, with the safeguards | [SUBPROCESSORS.md](SUBPROCESSORS.md); question 3 |
| Security measures | DPA Annex B |
| Retention | question 7 |
| Turnover and staff numbers | Your accounts (decides the fee tier) |

## 4. Apply

1. Go to the ODPC's online registration portal [VERIFY the current
   address, from the ODPC website].
2. Create an account for the entity, choose the capacity (processor,
   controller or both, if the portal allows one application for both
   [VERIFY]).
3. Fill in the form from section 3, upload the documents, and pay the fee.
   Fees are tiered by size [VERIFY current amounts].
4. Keep the reference number and the payment receipt.

**Check it worked:** the portal shows the application as approved and you
can download the certificate. Put the registration number in HouseMaster's
privacy notice and in the schools' data processing agreements.

## 5. After registering

- **Certificate validity:** the brief says two years [VERIFY].
- **Renewal reminder:** put a reminder in your calendar **three months
  before** the expiry date on the certificate, to renew in time.
- **Changes:** tell the ODPC when the registered details change (new
  purposes, new sub-processors abroad, a new address) [VERIFY how and by
  when].

## 6. The open question

Whether a vendor serving schools falls under the listed purpose of
operating an educational institution (and so cannot use the small-entity
exemption) is not spelled out [VERIFY]. Registering removes the risk either
way; ask counsel before relying on any exemption.
