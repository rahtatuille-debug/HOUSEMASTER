# Privacy notice template for schools using HouseMaster (draft)

*A school adapts this for its pupils, parents and staff. Fill in the
[brackets]; delete the parts about features the school doesn't use (for
example health notes, photos or the writing assistant). It describes only
what HouseMaster does for the school; the school's own notice may need to
cover more (admissions, fees, CCTV...). Everything marked [VERIFY] depends
on legal points counsel must confirm (QUESTIONS_FOR_COUNSEL.md).*

*The app shows parents and staff a privacy notice when they create their
account, and the school admin sets the contact shown there (Setup →
School settings → privacy contact). Link this notice from there.*

# How [School name] uses your information in HouseMaster

**Last updated:** [date]

## Who is responsible

[School legal name], [address], is responsible for the information about
pupils, parents and staff in HouseMaster (the "controller"). Contact:
[school privacy contact: name/role, email, phone].
[If appointed: our Data Protection Officer: name, contact.]

We use **HouseMaster**, run by [HouseMaster legal entity], to keep school
records and communicate with families. HouseMaster handles the information
only on our instructions, under a data processing agreement (it is our
"processor").

## What we keep, and why

| Whose | What | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Pupils | name, admission number, class, year group, house, date of birth, gender, nationality, dates of joining and leaving, mode of learning [, health notes] [, photo] | to run the school and know who is in each class [; health notes so staff can keep your child safe] |
| Pupils | grades, subject comments and targets, attendance, report cards | to teach, track progress and report to parents |
| Parents and guardians | name, email, phone numbers, relationship to the child, address, occupation, preferred way to contact you, which children you are linked to | so you can see your child's records and we can contact you |
| Staff | name, email, role, classes and subjects taught | to run the school and give staff access to their classes |
| Everyone who uses the app | messages, announcements you receive, urgent alerts and whether you have seen them, sign-in records, a log of changes made | to communicate, keep people safe, and keep a record of who changed what |

## Who can see what

- **Parents** see only their own children's records and reports (and only
  finalised reports), messages they are part of, and announcements and
  alerts sent to them.
- **Teachers** see the classes they teach. **Administrators** see the whole
  school.
- **Other parents** never see your details, except in a **class
  discussion**, where the parents taking part see each other's names.
  [VERIFY: QUESTIONS_FOR_COUNSEL.md question 8.]
- **Other schools** using HouseMaster can never see our information.

always reads and edits the draft before anything is sent to you.

Parents get emails when an announcement is published for them or a report

(emergencies such as a closure) are always sent by email as well as in the
app, even if you've turned other emails off.
